Why Can Tarot Generate Predictive Information Without Causal Power?

Nov. 9, 2026 · 10 min read

Tarot has no causal power over the events a reading is taken to describe. Turning over a card does not change a job offer, a relationship, or a deadline. And yet people consistently report that a reading carries information about their situation. This note asks where that information could come from when there is no causal channel to carry it.

The working hypothesis is synchronicity: a meaningful coincidence is treated as a structural feature of how an observer samples a situation, not as an anomaly to be explained away. The derivation treats a reading as a measurement — a mapping from a spread of cards to a set of statements — and asks which quantities decide whether those statements carry information about the question being asked.

What the derivation has to pin down

Three contributions are usually collapsed together: the base rate of the event being asked about, the prior the querent brings to the reading, and the interpretive freedom of the reader. The write-up defines the sample space of a spread, fixes what counts as a hit, and writes each contribution down explicitly before asking whether any predictive content is left over.

This is a working note, not a finished proof: the assumptions and the derivation are being written up, and no result is claimed yet. If you spot an error in the framing or want the argument pushed in a particular direction, write to vann.yufanzhou@gmail.com.
