Starting a research notebook

Nov. 5, 2026 · 1 min read

Most of what I read and run disappears within a week — a paper I skimmed, a run that diverged overnight, a bug that ate an afternoon. This page is where I keep the parts worth keeping.

The format is deliberately small. Short notes on papers: what the method actually does, what it measures, where the evaluation looks thin. Logs of reproductions: what broke, what fixed it, which numbers moved. Occasionally something longer, when a topic deserves more than a paragraph.

Writing in public is the point. A claim I would wave through in a private notebook has to survive being written in sentences someone else can read.

What will not appear here

No generated summaries posted unread. No numbers I have not run myself. Guesses are labelled as guesses.

There is no schedule. Entries are listed newest first on the blog page.
